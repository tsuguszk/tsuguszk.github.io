How to use the quiz

  • Each case is an ECG on one page followed by the answer on the next.
  • The later cases are difficult. However many times I try, I can't get them all right either.

Still, if you go through to the end, I think you will get a feel for the difference between WPW and FVP.

Key point

The key point is whether R is taller than S in leads V1 and V2.

Tests for a definite diagnosis

  • WPW: Holter monitoring is useful for diagnosing WPW.
  • FVP: the ATP test is the definitive way to diagnose FVP.
  • I think it is difficult to confirm FVP by Holter monitoring.
