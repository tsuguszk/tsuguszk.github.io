Since 2016 we have also used cryoablation, which treats by freezing (cryoablation alone in 155 procedures, combined with radiofrequency in 22).

Success and recurrence

"Success" means that tachycardia could no longer be induced at the end of the procedure, or that symptoms or the arrhythmia improved. "Recurrence" means that the same arrhythmia returned after the procedure.

Age at treatment

The most common ages are 12 to 14 (junior high school). We treat patients from infants to adults we have followed since childhood.

Body weight at treatment

131 procedures were in small children weighing under 15 kg (roughly up to 3 years of age). The smaller the body, the harder it is to handle the catheters.

Methods: from our department's ablation database, we counted all 1,723 records (1,491 patients) in which radiofrequency or cryo energy was delivered for an arrhythmia between January 1, 2006 and September 26, 2026. Repeat procedures in the same patient were each counted as one procedure. Recurrence is the number recorded as recurrence in the database (the length of follow-up was not standardized).
