2022.08.28

My first Norikura Hill Climb!

Climbing Mt. Norikura by bike had been one of my dreams since I started riding a road bike ten years ago. I had visited with my children on a summer holiday, and this time the dream came true at a big event with about 3,700 entrants. I hoped to make it up in about two hours without putting a foot down, but after 16 km I had to put my foot down five or six times and reached the top exhausted, resting along the way.

My time was 2 hours 38 minutes. With 5 m to go I couldn't pedal any more and finished hopping on one foot. A sad ending.

I may have got in the way of serious riders chasing their times, but it was a precious and fun experience. Thank you to all the staff for a great time.

Filmed with a GoPro 10 for the first time. 4K, 30 fps.

2021.03.27

To the Sewari-tei embankment in Yawata, with the cherry blossoms in full bloom

During the COVID-19 pandemic, for the first time in a year and four months, I rode 30 km from the hospital to the Sewari-tei embankment in Yawata, famous for its cherry blossoms, with Dr. Yoshihide Nakamura, Dr. Fukudome and Dr. Terashi.

Dr. Fukudome and Dr. Terashi were beginners. Dr. Nakamura brought three bikes from his home; Dr. Fukudome rode a cross bike and Dr. Terashi an electric cross bike.

Fine weather and cherry blossoms in full bloom. A lovely ride after a long break.

2019.11.16

To Jusan Pass with Dr. Shoda

Dr. Shoda came to Osaka for work in Nara, so I took him on a ride to Jusan Pass on the border between Nara and Osaka. I picked him up at Itami Airport and we rode across Osaka City to the pass. Riding behind me, Dr. Shoda later gave me lots of advice on how I ride. I usually ride alone, so I learned a lot. About two minutes into the climb Dr. Shoda was out of sight, but once again I made it to the top. Thank you, Dr. Shoda, for coming to my home climb!

The ride was 93 km in total, but clearly harder than the 150 km around Awaji Island... With Dr. Shoda behind me, I pushed too hard for the first 30 km or so to the pass.

2019.09.23

The 10th Awaji Island Long Ride 150

I rode "Awaichi", the loop around Awaji Island I had wanted to do since I started cycling, with 2,300 riders.

The Nara ride has 100 riders, so the scale was completely different. After a small mishap I restarted last, 2,300th, from the 30 km point, but I managed to finish in about nine hours.

It was a fun ride.

2019.06.02

The 10th Nara Basin Cyclo Rally

My fourth time at the annual "Nara Basin Cyclo Rally 140 km". This year the course ended with two mountain crossings: 146 km, with exhausting climbs. Start at 8:00, finish at 16:20. It was tough.

Dr. Shoda and Dr. Kioka finished first again, and were first over the last two climbs (Mt. Yoshino and Imo Pass). They are now famous at this event.

Dr. Eitoku was fast too. He left me behind at the end, but I finished only about 10 minutes later.

After an ablation meeting of the Japanese Heart Rhythm Society in Okinawa, I rode an 87 km loop around the south of the island.

2018.06.03

Nara Basin Cyclo Rally (my third time)

My third "Nara Basin Cyclo Rally 140 km", actually a 150 km course. Start at 8:00, finish at 16:00. It was tough.

100 riders took part in the 150 km, and I heard 57% finished... Dr. Shoda finished first!!! I seem to have been around 25th to 30th, about the middle of the finishers? I am not that slow; the HBS team is just too fast.

This year Dr. Eitoku of Okayama University joined for the first time. We thought he was a beginner, but he turned out to be amazingly powerful, and everyone was surprised.

2016.05 / 2017

Nara Basin Cyclo Rally: my first ride and my revenge

May 2016: the "Nara Basin Cyclo Rally" 130 km, my first time, invited by Dr. Shoda

I couldn't keep up with Dr. Shoda and Dr. Nakano and rode alone from halfway... By the end my legs wouldn't move at all, and I couldn't walk properly for a week afterwards.

The next year, 2017: the Nara Basin Cyclo Rally 140 km. My revenge.

The 140 km course: from Nara Park to Yagyu village, south through the mountains, through Yoshino to Asuka, and finally north through the city.

Up and down, again and again. Elevation gain 1,500 m! Detailed data
